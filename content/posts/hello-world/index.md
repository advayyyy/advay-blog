Welcome to my blog. This is the first post, so let me say a little about why it exists.

I wanted a place to keep my notes as I learn — the rough version, written while I'm still figuring things out. Writing things down forces me to understand them properly, and if someone else finds them useful along the way, even better.

## What to expect

Posts here will mostly be:

- short notes on things I'm learning
- small project write-ups
- the occasional longer essay when something really clicks

No fixed schedule — I'll write when I have something worth saying.

## How this is built

This site is a static [Astro](https://astro.build) blog. It's fast, self-contained, and every page is just markdown, so writing feels like writing.
